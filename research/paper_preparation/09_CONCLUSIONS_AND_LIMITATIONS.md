# Conclusions and limits

## Conclusions supported now

The original high fit does not establish useful future forecasting: contemporaneous target information enters the original feature construction, and corrected forecast evaluations require strong persistence controls. Existing reproduced RF specifications perform worse than monthly persistence on the inspected historical sample. This does not prove that all nonlinear models or macro features fail.

The latest daily spread substantially improves the exploratory next-month mean benchmark relative to the previous monthly mean. This is consistent with established aggregation theory. Its magnitude in this dataset is useful evidence, but neither statistical significance nor operational real-time availability has been established by that comparison alone.

A raw current-spread ranking outperforms the inspected RF stress-state ranking at all four horizons after fixing unknown future labels. Because that diagnostic retains a full-sample threshold, it is a motivation for the frozen-threshold study rather than its final result.

The frozen legacy threshold produces only three post-2008 entries. Many positive overlapping forecast windows do not create many independent stress events. The existing evidence cannot support a claim of reliable advance crisis warning.

## Conditional conclusion language for the completed study

| Future evidence | Defensible conclusion |
|---|---|
| Regime gain positive with useful magnitude, stable uncertainty and external support | Regime representation improves the specified target under the documented information set |
| Gain disappears after adding latest spread | Earlier apparent gain was sensitive to the benchmark/information set; describe the measured contrast, not a universal causal attribution |
| Interval rules out the chosen 5% gain | The study rules out gains of that magnitude for this specification under its inference assumptions |
| Interval spans meaningful gains and losses | Evidence is inconclusive; do not write “no predictive information” |
| State skill is strong but entries are missed | State discrimination does not translate into adequate warning in the evaluated events |
| Historical result fails on extension | Scope the conclusion to the historical period and discuss instability explicitly |

## Limitations that belong in the paper

One aggregate spread series; 309 monthly observations; revised macro releases; assumed daily/monthly availability; a mislabeled Euro-area series requiring correction; very few entries; overlapping outcomes and serial dependence; retrospective choices informed by the inspected sample; threshold sensitivity; and source licensing/redistribution terms to verify before publishing data.

Regime membership is a statistical representation, not identification of a structural economic mechanism. Forecast improvement does not establish causation. OAS prediction is not default-risk calibration, portfolio utility, or trading profitability. Additional time periods and correlated indices improve robustness but do not automatically add independent crises.

## Recommended decision

Proceed as a focused empirical research note. Finish the matched information/ablation study and one frozen external check before expanding the model family. If effect estimates remain imprecise and the extension is unavailable, present a transparent exploratory case study with reproducible negative/inconclusive evidence rather than a general forecasting breakthrough.
